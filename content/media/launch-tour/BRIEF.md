## Intent

A fast product tour of SpecKit Companion for r/SpecDrivenDevelopment, LinkedIn, X and spec-kit's Show and tell. It must feel like a real product demo: many real screens, quick cuts (two to four seconds a shot), every feature from the brochure. The first attempt was too slow and too thin; this one shows the product working. Captions only, no voice, because feeds autoplay muted.

## Assets

All under `capture/assets/`, every one a real capture of the product in Bearded Theme Light, listed in `capture/extracted/asset-descriptions.md`. Feature the captured screens as the video's assets; do not rebuild the UI.

## Customizations

- Silent: no music, no sound. Confirmed by the user 2026-10-26.
- The plan components are published, not work in progress. Only the workflow builder is "coming next".

- Show-it-as-is: feature the product's own captured screens and recordings.
- Cover what the brochure covers: the viewer, stories and scenarios, tasks, the footer that runs each step, comments and Refine, the Overview (intent and timing, expectations, verified, decisions, coverage), the sidebar's five panes, living specs, the workflow builder, the four plan components, the Copilot app, the Claude Code mod.
- Constellation look: near-black violet ground, violet accent, Figtree. Specky the mascot only at the open or the close.
